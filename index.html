Life at Calibri

Mornings start in the kitchen. The open layout means you can pour coffee, scramble eggs, and chat across the counter while someone else is on the couch reading the news. The east-facing windows in the living rooms light the whole downstairs by 8am.

Walks at Copperfield. Two minutes out the front door and you're at the park — long enough to clear your head between meetings, short enough that the dog doesn't get used to a real outing.

Weeknight dinners with friends. Five bedrooms means you've got room to host without juggling. Wine on the patio, the grill going, kids running between living rooms.

Domain weekends. Ten minutes to dinner reservations, shopping, the movies. You're close enough to drop in spontaneously and far enough to come home to quiet.
